### Taxonomy Guide



###### Motivation:

This taxonomy guide was made to annotate (categorize) the articles about Mamdani.



|Category|Definition|Example|out-cases|
|-|-|-|-|
|Political Conflict|Any article about two political actors in conflict.|A politician publicly criticizing another politician or political party.<br /><br />Disagreements between members of the same political party.<br /><br />Conflicts between a politician and another country's government.<br /><br />Political disputes involving legislation, policy decisions, or government actions.<br /><br />A politician responding to accusations or criticism from a political opponent.|A politician criticizing a non-political organization without a political opponent being involved.<br /><br />General discussions about political disagreements without identifiable actors in conflict.<br /><br />Articles discussing ideological differences without an active disagreement or confrontation.<br /><br />A politician announcing a policy that happens to oppose another party's position, without an actual dispute.|
|Campaigning|Any event or action done by the politician to gain electoral support.|A politician holding rallies, campaign events, or public speeches to attract voters.<br /><br />Announcements of campaign promises or electoral platforms.<br /><br />Fundraising activities and campaign donations.<br /><br />Campaign advertisements, slogans, and promotional materials.<br /><br />A politician visiting communities or constituencies as part of an electoral campaign.<br /><br />Discussions of campaign strategy, polling, and voter outreach.|A politician attending a public event unrelated to an election campaign.<br /><br />Routine government announcements made while the politician is in office.<br /><br />General political commentary about a politician's popularity without reference to their campaign.<br /><br />A politician discussing policy in an official government capacity rather than seeking electoral support.<br /><br />Public engagement that is unrelated to an upcoming or ongoing election.|
|Ideology|Any article that criticizes/approves of the politician's ideologies/worldviews.|Articles describing a politician as conservative, liberal, socialist, nationalist, or progressive.<br /><br />Criticism of a politician's ideological beliefs or political philosophy.<br /><br />Articles praising a politician for adhering to particular ideological principles.<br /><br />Discussions of how a politician's beliefs influence their policy positions.<br /><br />Comparisons between a politician's ideology and that of their political party.<br /><br />Articles examining ideological shifts or changes in a politician's beliefs.|Articles discussing a specific policy without connecting it to the politician's broader beliefs or ideology.<br /><br />Reports about a politician implementing a policy without ideological analysis.<br /><br />Political disagreements that concern practical decisions rather than underlying beliefs.<br /><br />Articles discussing a political party's ideology without relating it to the politician.<br /><br />Descriptions of a politician's actions without examining the beliefs behind them.|
|Controversy|Articles that report on, investigate, or discuss a disputed, scandalous, ethically questionable, or publicly contentious event, statement, decision, or action involving the politician|Allegations of corruption, misconduct, abuse of power, or unethical behavior.<br /><br />A politician making a statement that generates significant public backlash.<br /><br />Scandals involving a politician's personal or professional conduct.<br /><br />Investigations into questionable decisions or actions by a politician.<br /><br />Public outrage or criticism surrounding a politician's behavior.<br /><br />A politician being implicated in a legal or ethical dispute, even if the allegations remain unproven.|Ordinary political disagreements that do not involve a scandal or contentious incident.<br /><br />Routine criticism of a politician's policies or ideological positions.<br /><br />Articles discussing allegations that do not involve the politician directly.<br /><br />Minor mistakes or routine administrative errors that do not generate meaningful controversy.<br /><br />General negative coverage of a politician without reference to a specific contentious issue.|
|Leadership|Any article about the politician taking action and making changes to society.|A politician introducing major legislative or institutional reforms.<br /><br />Announcements of new government initiatives or national development plans.<br /><br />A politician responding to a national crisis or emergency in a leadership capacity.<br /><br />Decisions involving public administration, governance, or institutional restructuring.<br /><br />A politician taking action to address societal challenges.<br /><br />Discussions evaluating the effectiveness or failures of a politician's leadership.<br /><br />A politician negotiating or making decisions on behalf of their government.|A politician expressing an opinion or making a promise without taking concrete action.<br /><br />Campaign speeches about what the politician intends to do if elected.<br /><br />Articles focusing on disagreements between political actors rather than governance.<br /><br />Routine appearances or public interactions without an exercise of leadership.<br /><br />Articles discussing a politician's popularity or influence without examining their leadership actions.|
|Influence|Any article about the politician's influence about a particular topic.|A politician influencing public opinion on a particular issue.<br /><br />Articles examining how a politician shapes national or international policy debates.<br /><br />A politician influencing other politicians, political parties, or government institutions.<br /><br />Discussions of a politician's impact on social movements or public discourse.<br /><br />A politician's role in influencing international relations or diplomatic decisions.<br /><br />Articles examining the politician's growing or declining political influence.<br /><br />A politician serving as a role model or symbolic figure for a particular movement.|Articles describing a politician's formal authority without discussing their broader impact.<br /><br />Routine policy announcements or government decisions better categorized as Leadership.<br /><br />Articles discussing a politician's personal opinions without showing their impact on others.<br /><br />Articles discussing popularity without demonstrating an effect on public opinion or political outcomes.<br /><br />Articles about a politician attending an event without any meaningful influence being discussed.|
|Media Criticism|Any article that criticizes how media sources showcase the politician|A politician accusing news outlets of biased or unfair reporting.<br /><br />Articles discussing media misrepresentation of a politician's statements or actions.<br /><br />Criticism of how journalists frame stories involving a politician.<br /><br />Allegations that media organizations deliberately omit important context.<br /><br />Articles examining whether a politician receives disproportionate negative or positive media coverage.<br /><br />Discussions of censorship, misinformation, or selective reporting involving coverage of the politician.<br /><br />A politician criticizing the media's treatment of their political movement or administration.|Articles that simply report on negative coverage of a politician without criticizing the media itself.<br /><br />Articles criticizing a politician's use of media or communication strategies.<br /><br />Articles discussing disagreements between politicians and journalists where the central issue is not media representation.<br /><br />General criticism of the media that does not relate to the politician.<br /><br />Articles discussing a politician's social media activity without examining media portrayal.|
|Public Engagement|Any article about public events the politician participates in that engages with the normal populace.|A politician visiting local communities or neighborhoods.<br /><br />Meetings with residents to discuss local concerns.<br /><br />Town halls, public consultations, and community forums.<br /><br />A politician interacting with citizens during public events.<br /><br />Visits to schools, hospitals, workplaces, or community organizations involving direct public engagement.<br /><br />A politician participating in charitable or community activities.<br /><br />Public question-and-answer sessions or listening tours.|Private meetings with other politicians, government officials, or political donors.<br /><br />Closed-door diplomatic meetings.<br /><br />Campaign rallies where the primary purpose is electoral promotion (Campaigning).<br /><br />Press conferences primarily intended to communicate with journalists.<br /><br />Ceremonial appearances where there is no meaningful interaction with the public.<br /><br />Online statements or social media posts that do not involve direct public engagement.|
|VIDEO|Articles that are videos (not really a category, more to quick search)|if is a video only|if it's not a video|
|SATIRE|Articles that are satirical (not really a category, more to quick search)|if is satire|if not satire|
